About OrderBanao

OrderBanao is a simple order and delivery manager built for small businesses, traders, shopkeepers and home sellers.

What it does

  • Create multi-item customer orders with prices, delivery fees and discounts.
  • Record partial payments and calculate balances automatically.
  • Update delivery status manually from Pending to Delivered.
  • Print order slips, manage customer details, and view sales reports.
  • Export CSV records, restore JSON backups and switch between English/Urdu.

No online courier tracking or payment gateway is provided.
