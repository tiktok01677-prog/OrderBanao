How to Delete Your Data

OrderBanao stores business and customer records locally in your browser or Android WebView storage. The provided website does not offer account registration.

Delete all app records

  1. Open OrderBanao on the device containing your records.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete all records and confirm.

This deletes customer, order and payment records stored by OrderBanao on that device. App preferences are retained.

Remove stored site data entirely

You can also clear the site's storage through browser settings. In an Android WebView app, clearing app storage or uninstalling may delete the local records and preferences. Back up first if you need a copy.

Downloaded copies

Previously downloaded JSON or CSV files, printed slips and records shared with others are not deleted by the app. Delete those copies separately.

Request help

Contact YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com for questions. Because the provided app does not run a developer database for your records, the developer cannot access or delete records stored only on your device.
