Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

OrderBanao is a local-first order and payment record app. Review this policy against the exact Android WebView build before submitting to Google Play.

Information entered by you

You may enter business name, phone and address, customer names, phone numbers and addresses, order item names, dates, prices, payment amounts and notes. This may include personal information about your customers. You are responsible for having an appropriate reason to record it.

Where information is stored

The website stores these records in your browser's or Android WebView's local storage on your device. The provided app source does not send customer orders or payments to a developer-operated server. There is no account registration or automatic cloud backup.

How data is used

The app uses your records to calculate totals and balances, show delivery and payment status, create printable order slips and generate downloads or backup files.

Sharing and hosting

OrderBanao's provided website source contains no advertising SDK or analytics script. Hosting providers such as GitHub Pages may process access requests and associated technical data (such as IP addresses and request details) according to their own policies. If you export, print, share or email records, the receiving service or person may access that information.

Security and retention

Local storage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to the same device and app storage may be able to read the records. Lock your device, safeguard downloaded backups and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details. The app retains local records until you delete them, clear app/site storage or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove them.

Backups, deletion and control

From Settings you can export JSON backups and CSV files, import an earlier backup or delete all local records. Deleting app records does not delete copies you have downloaded or shared. See the data deletion instructions.

Children

OrderBanao is a business record tool, not a product specifically directed to children.
